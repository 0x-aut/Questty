# Project brief: Questty

## The problem
People with a full head lose the thread. Tasks live in five apps, the room is full of distractions, and starting is the hardest part.

## What Questty does
Questty turns the room you are sitting in into a calm place to get things done. Pinch and say what is on your mind; the buddy turns it into quest cards. Pin each card where it belongs: the desk for work, the door for things to take with you, a wall for later.

## Goals for this sprint
- Read documents in the room without leaving the headset.
- A short welcome back that shows what is waiting.
- Finishing should feel physical: crumple the card, toss it on the shelf.
- Accessibility first: seated, one-handed and reduced-motion modes.

## Non-goals
- No new AI models. Everything runs on the existing buddy actions.
- No room growth. The room stays yours.

## Success looks like
1. A first-time user pins three cards in under two minutes.
2. A returning user knows what is waiting within ten seconds.
3. Nobody finishes a card by accident.

## Risks
Hand tracking varies between people and lighting. Every gesture needs a calm fallback: a button, a voice command, or both.

## Timeline
Week 1: documents and the welcome back. Week 2: physical finish and tiers. Week 3: accessibility pass and a real headset test.
