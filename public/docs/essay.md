# My essay: Why small rooms make big work

## Opening
The best work I have ever done happened in small rooms. Not because they were quiet, but because everything in them was there for a reason.

## A room is a to-do list
When my notes live on the wall, I see them without opening anything. A card on the door reminds me before I leave. A card on the desk asks to be done now. The room does the remembering so my head does not have to.

## Attention is spatial
We remember where things are better than what they are called. Putting a task in a place gives it a home, and a home makes it easier to come back to.

## The case for finishing things physically
Ticking a box is forgettable. Crumpling a note and tossing it is not. The small ritual tells the brain that the loop is closed.

## What gets in the way
Too many tools, too many tabs, and the feeling that everything is equally urgent. A calm room with a few visible priorities beats an infinite list.

## Closing
Make the room small enough to see and kind enough to come back to. Then do one thing, finish it, and let it go.
