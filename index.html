How it works

No typing and no menus to learn. Talk, place, focus, flick.

  1. Brain dump by voice

    Pinch the orb and say everything on your mind. AI splits it into quest cards with XP.

  2. Pin cards to your room

    Grab a card and stick it to a wall, your desk or the door, where it stays next time.

  3. Focus session

    Your room frosts over, a visual timer counts down and your buddy works quietly beside you.

  4. Flick it done

    Flick a finished card into the trash can. It crumples, lands, and you earn XP.

Where to run it

Best on Meta Quest 3 or 3S in Quest Browser. On desktop, Chrome with Meta's Immersive Web Emulator works too.

Allow the microphone

Questty listens only while you hold a pinch on the orb. When the browser asks for the mic, choose Allow, or you can still pick quick quest templates.
